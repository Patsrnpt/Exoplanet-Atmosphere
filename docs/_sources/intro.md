# Introduction

This site hosts a Jupyter Notebook tutorial on using cross-correlation to study exoplanet atmospheres: detecting molecular composition and measuring horizontal wind speed from high-resolution spectra, and seeing how instrument resolution affects what you can recover.

The tutorial was developed by **Sarunyapat (Pat) Phoompuang** (Imperial College London) under the guidance of **Dr. Hayley Beltz** (University of Kansas), as part of ongoing research at the University of Maryland, College Park. For background on the research this tutorial supports, see the [project repository](https://github.com/Patsrnpt/Exoplanet-Atmosphere).

---

## What You'll Learn

- The mathematical foundation of the cross-correlation function and how it applies to exoplanet spectra.
- How to prepare observed and template spectra for cross-correlation: wavelength conversion, resolution detrending, and converting absorption spectra to emission format.
- How to use cross-correlation with molecular templates to detect atmospheric composition.
- How to estimate horizontal wind speed in an exoplanet's atmosphere using cross-correlation.
- How instrument resolution affects the accuracy of both composition and wind speed measurements.
